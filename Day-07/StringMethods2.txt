Whitespace and Trimming Methods:
1.strip()-it is going to remove the spaces on both sides of the string
2.lstrip()-it is going to remove the spaces on left side
3.rstrip()-it is going to remove the spaces on right side
4.replace()-going to remove in between spaces also
Splitting and joining Methods
1.split()-it is going to split based on the occurrence  
2.rsplit()-it is going to split from right side
3.splitlines()-if we have lines it will split
4.join()-joins the string
5.partition()-it will be partition into right side, left side, and middle
6.rpartition()-it will be partition into right side, left side, and middle
 from right side
String Testing Methods
1.startswith()-checks whether the string starts with or not
2.endswith()-checks whether the string ends with it or not
3.isalpha()-checks all the characters should be alphabets
4.isalnum()-checks all the characters should be numeric and alphabets 
5.isspace()-checks only the empty space
6.istitle()-checks the title case
7.isupper()-checks all the characters are upper or not
8.islower()-checks all the characters are lower or not
9.isidentifier()-checks whether the identifier is valid or not
10.isdecimal()-checks whether only the decimals
11.isdigit()-used when we have subscript and superscript
12.isnumeric()-used when we use roman numbers
